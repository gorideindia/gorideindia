Who We Are

GoRide is a self-drive car rental platform built with a simple idea — give people the freedom to travel on their own terms. We are building a technology-driven mobility platform that connects customers with quality vehicles for convenient, flexible and transparent travel.

Our goal is to make car rental simple, accessible and trustworthy while creating opportunities for car owners to participate in the growing mobility ecosystem.
